About Q-io | 主理人 24hr-168

Q-io is an independent landscape observer and creator of micro-literature. This site, 24hr-168, serves as a pure textual field archive dedicated to on-site investigations of Taiwan's borderland landscapes, historical trails, and community living spaces. Rejecting algorithm-driven platforms, we document memories through a structured three-part framework: geographical coordinates, field essays, spatial notes, and flash fiction.
